Figure 3. Spearman correlations between response time (RT) on the five in-game test tasks and the standardized cognitive measures at posttest. Each column is one in-game task at its shared difficulty level (ColorJudgement 13, Singler 8, MatchCard 12, Pack 8, Toilet 18); RT is the mean per-trial response time of the participant’s non-passive sessions at that level. The analysis includes the 94 participants with at least one non-passive session at the shared level of all five tasks (n = 86–93 per pair, depending on missing standardized scores). Cell values are Spearman ρ; colour encodes ρ. Asterisks mark uncorrected p values (* p < .05, ** p < .01, *** p < .001). After Benjamini–Hochberg correction across all 25 pairs, the smallest pFDR is .070 (MatchCard–N-back 2-vs-0 RT); no correlation survives FDR correction.
